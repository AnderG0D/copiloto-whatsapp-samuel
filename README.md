# Copiloto WhatsApp Samuel

A NestJS backend that helps a sales team review incoming WhatsApp conversations and prepare response drafts. It receives events through Evolution API, applies business rules, and stores authorized information in Supabase. Drafts are generated through a provider-neutral AI interface.

An authenticated admin panel lets a reviewer inspect, edit, approve, or reject drafts. AI proposes content; business decisions and effects remain under backend and human control.
